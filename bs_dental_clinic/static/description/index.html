Core Features

Patient Management

Full patient profiles with medical history, allergies, blood group, gender, emergency contacts, and auto-created Odoo partner.

Appointments

Schedule and track appointments per doctor. Integrates with Odoo's native Appointment module for online booking.

Treatment Plans

Multi-procedure treatment records with tooth chart, unit price, discount, tax, and a full Sales Order → Invoice → Treatment flow.

Prescriptions

Issue digital prescriptions with medicine, dosage, frequency, and duration. Print professional PDF Rx slips with doctor signature.

X-Ray Records

Attach and manage patient X-Ray images and diagnostic notes directly linked to treatments and appointments.

Tooth Chart

FDI-numbered tooth system with surface mapping (Mesial, Distal, Buccal, Lingual, Occlusal) for precise clinical records.

Treatment Invoice PDF

Print a branded invoice/receipt directly from any treatment. Shows procedures, taxes, totals, amount due, and ✓ PAID stamp.

Inventory Tracking

Track 35+ pre-loaded dental products. Record usage per treatment and auto-consume stock via Odoo's native stock.scrap flow.

Staff & HR Sync

Doctors and staff automatically sync to Odoo HR Employees. Check-in / check-out creates real hr.attendance records.
